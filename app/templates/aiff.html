Scope — what AIFF on the DPU inspects

Every flow between the two netns sides above traverses the DPU and is inspected by all three lanes in order: Access Control decides whether the flow is allowed at all; Anti-Virus inspects file content on accepted flows; IPS inspects the accepted, clean stream for exploit and scan signatures. Traffic that never crosses the DPU (same-subnet lateral movement, the GPU backend fabric) is not inspected by any of the three. A port scan is Detect-only by design — the IPS lane logs it but does not drop it.
